      The design board

      Every screen a live phone, before a line of Swift.

      The design-board skill ships a template: every screen of the app as a working 390 × 844 phone in rows, the kit in its own view, Pick and Note on every card, and Play the flow, full screen. Review it with your thumb, then port what you approved.

      iammirrr.github.io/ungenerated/board
      • Live phones. Every card plays its own demo; the whole-part card plays a section in order.
      • Pick and Note. Choose between variations and leave notes on the screens themselves.
      • Play the flow. Walk the journey full screen with the real transitions.
      • Kit, research, transitions. Each in its own view, in the order of the work.
