      The design board

      Every screen a live phone, before a line of Swift.

      The design-board skill ships a template: every screen of the app as a working 390 × 844 phone, a row per flow with a card that plays it whole, the kit and the motion styles in their own view, Pick and Note on every card, and Play the flow, full screen.

      iammirrr.github.io/ungenerated/board

      • Pick and Note. Choose between variations and leave notes on the screens themselves.
