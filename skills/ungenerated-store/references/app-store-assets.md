# App Store assets

The short checklist this skill started from. Where it differs, `references/screenshots.md` and
`references/trailer.md` win: screenshots use straight phones where they help, and the preview is motion graphics in
one movement, not the opening replayed.

## Screenshots

1. **Five to eight shots**, in the order of the opening's story: the promise, the hands-on moment,
   the product at work, everywhere it lives, the result.
2. **One message per shot**: a 3–6 word caption above a real screen from the board.
3. Use the board's `#only-<id>` view and its `tools/capture.mjs` (`ungenerated-flows` template) at the store's sizes
   (iPhone 6.9" 1320 × 2868, 6.5" 1284 × 2778; Android 1080 × 1920 or larger).
4. Same ground and type as the app. No device frames unless the platform asks for them.
5. Real data that matches the captions.

## Preview video

- 15–30 seconds, the opening's beats played in the app or on the board, no voice-over needed.
- The first two seconds must make sense with the sound off.
- Capture frames with the board's `tools/capture.mjs` at a high rate or record the screen; cut on beat changes.

## Rules

- Never show features the app does not have yet.
- No awards, ratings or press quotes you cannot show the source for.
- Captions follow `ungenerated-foundation` › human copy (`references/human-copy.md`).
