# What the owner taught, round by round (Comma, 8 Nov 2026)

Each lesson is a rule in `references/screenshots.md`; the transferable ones are also in `references/lessons.md`. The quotes are the owner's (dictated).

| Round | What was made | What he said | The rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A template system: colour swaps, 3D props from an image model, cut-out cards, frames animating | "SOO ASS" · "did you use the codecs to make images" · "why the screenshots are moving" | No props or pasted cards; still frames; variations must be real directions |
| 2 | Huge phones turned 20–38°, pushed into the feature | "EXTREME TILTING ... NO FEATURE IS SHOWING ... I LIKED THE STRAIGHT PHONES" | Straight phones, subtle tilt; the feature readable |
| 3 | Straight phones with UI lifted out | "YOU NEED SHOW THE FEATURE AND TEXT SHOULD EXACTLY BE SHOWING THAT CORE THING" | The picture says exactly what the headline says |
| 4 | Feature blocks, 21 concepts | "WHY THE FUCK THESE ARE SOOO ASSS ... CAN WE THINK ABOUT IT" | Stop mass-producing; one frame at a time, by hand, judged at full size |
| 5 | Header layouts, emoji, focus fade | "no shadow the typography need to be consistent" · "use phone in one, other can be more independent" | One headline style; phones optional; brand and white mixed |
| 6 | The app's onboarding language | "LOOKS ASS" | Compose like the best reference, not like the app's own pages |
| 7 | Seven screenshots by hand | "this one looks a lot better" | The method works: keep it |
| 8 | Click-to-edit | "genuinely impressed by the fact that I can edit stuff by just clicking" | Every piece editable; several at once; opacity; arrows |
| 9 | Practice screenshot | "MOTION BLUR ETC" | Show movement where the feature moves |
| 10 | Refine screenshot | "I LOVE THIS SCREEN SHOT THIS ISNT ADVERTISING OUR MAIN PRODUCT" | Every screenshot sells the core |
| 11 | Raw app captures in phones | "it's amazing here" · then: trim it, bigger text, dim the phone, pop the card out, "I didn't mean really actual, you still have creative freedom" | Edited true-to-app screens (screenshots.md §6) |
| 12 | A/B per slot, every size, iPad, languages | "select A/B versions of both" · "respecting the proportions" · in-app words in the native language, "but I don't think it should advertise any script writing in a native language" | Pick per slot; sizes and iPad keep proportions; translate the interface, not what people write |
| 13 | Every language and size | "it's kind of leaking out ... touching the borders ... the Japanese one ... worst" · "USE GOOD NICE FONTS" | A font per script and role, glyphs inside the pill, side margins |
| 14 | iPads, MacBooks, per-model iPhones | "IN IPAD SCREENSHOT USE IPADS" · "make things bigger and nicer for both iPads" · "5.5 ... too old make it like iPhone 12ish" | The device follows the canvas; iPads use their space |
| 15 | The owner auditing by eye | "iPad have a lot of [space] for the heading text, but it is small ... these are the little things that you need to audit ... add that in the skills" | audit.mjs on every page, size and language before showing; iPad pages per slot when the automatic one is weak |

Other things he cared about: big bold type, never small text; reels that look like reels (tall, likes, handle); a
different real example on each screenshot; the app's own icons ("I hate the icon of the stars"); cards on one line;
the character still; HD pictures; a true-to-app version under each, from the real app.
