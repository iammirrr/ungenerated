# Lessons: five cuts of Comma's trailer (8 Nov 2026)

Each cut, what the owner said, and what it taught. His words are quoted as he dictated them.

## Cut 1: an HTML page, the screenshots' phones sliding past

"FROM THE APP AND FEATURE CAN YOU MAKE EXTREMELY DYNAMIC TRAILER". The first cut put frame shake, white flashes, circle
wipes, words that rotated in on overshoot and a hard whip on every scene into 29 s.
**"THE TRAIL NEED EXTREME REFINEMENT YOU MADE SUCH A ASS MOTION DESIGNING."**
- No shake, no flashes, no wipes, no wobble, nothing spinning. Smooth eased moves that land once.

## Cut 2: one phone, the real screens driven inside it

Rebuilt before he saw it, when he said: **"THE TRAILER SHOULD NICE LIKE COMPACT AND LIKE PRODUCT SAAS TRAILER NOT SOME AI
GENERATED CRAP"**, and then pointed at github.com/noamdorr/saas-product-demo-video: **"USE THIS AND PLEASE MAKE FULL AGAIN"**.

## Cut 3 (Remotion, the demo-video skill): "this one looks really good"

A dark/cream/dark/orange tonal arc, big serif headlines, a reel breaking into its beats, cuts on the beat.
**"looks really good, but ... it is not exaggerating the features a lot ... in the first frame I would like to add a lot
more motion ... these reels moving in a really skewed way ... one of these reels got into the phone and then smoothly
transition ... I like this part where it's hook ... the shape ... the text at the top is supposed to be like the same as
the screenshots"**, and a story: scripts from a shape, or a brain dump, a format, the teleprompter you adjust, record,
grammar, takes, all in one.
- The signature move (a reel into its beats) is the keeper.
- Headlines in the screenshots' type.

## Cut 4: the screenshots animated

Every scene was laid out as a screenshot: cards lifted out of phones, scissors, flying filler pills, chips and arrows.
**"THIS TRAILER IS LITERALLY THE SCREENSHOT COMPILED, I'M DISAPPOINTED TO MY CORE, IT'S NOT SMOOTH OR TRANSITIONING
BETTER, OVERLY PACED, CLUTTERED WITH USELESS STUFF."** Then: **"I don't know why you are taking Yoniman's reel so much. You
can use anything ... I want it to feel like an actual app but things are getting highlighted, the AI feature getting
highlighted ... I don't want you to make the screenshots as a trailer."** And, offered a phone walkthrough instead:
**"NOT AT ALL, I WANT TO MAKE MOTION GRAPHIC A LOT MORE NICER."**
- The trailer is never the screenshots. It's never a phone walkthrough either. It is motion graphics.
- Vary the content.
- Highlight the AI.

## Cut 5: motion graphics, one movement

The checkpoint (the opening alone) was sent first:
- Reels on a tilted plane, rows gliding against each other.
- *Real or cake?* lifting off, straightening and playing.
- The card splitting into four slices that become its beats.
- The beats taking the writer's words in green.

**"I like the start, I really do like the start ... this one is like the best starting ... I really like the shape."**
There was one bug: the previous headline's pill peeked out under the next one. Then he asked for more: **"it doesn't do
the AI stuff ... doesn't showcase the complete writer ... a little bit longer and keep advertising the app that you're not
covering in the screenshot ... Exaggerate the features of like brain dump ... more connected ... all the learnings you have
to update in the skill."**

The rest was built so each part grows out of the last:
1. The beats merge into the brain-dump card, where the words land as they are said.
2. On the track's lift, Comma's light runs round the dump and its questions come one at a time.
3. Three drafts write themselves and one comes forward.
4. The chosen draft opens into the writer: read time, a beat held and moved, Mark ready.
5. The page goes dark into the teleprompter, and the takes count up.
6. Everything folds into the icon.

29.0 s.

## Rules that fell out of it

- **Build a checkpoint before the film.** Twice a whole film went out on a feel he rejected. Ten seconds would have shown it.
- **Check each hand-over as a pair of frames.** Four of six cuts first dropped something (a mic, a question, a page)
  between a part's last frame and the next one's first.
- **Promise only what the app does.** It has no video editing, so the film never says "edit".
- **Exaggerate the feature, not the decoration around it.**
- **Ask early what the screenshots don't cover.** That's what the trailer is for.

## Cut 6: "nearly perfect", then six asks

**"this trailer is nearly perfect"**. The asks:
- **The highlighted word.** It popped in small and tilted. Now it rides up with its line while its pill fills in behind it.
- **The AI animation, then typing.** Every AI moment wears the same running light round the card, then the words
  *type* in. A sweep band and a roll read as a different effect.
- **"How do you want to write it?"** Show it and play both paths: borrow a creator's format, and the app writes it.
- **The drafts' entrance looked weird.** Glows behind half-written cards fanning in from the edges. They now rise
  neatly from below into a tight fan inside the frame, with one light round the front card.
- **Shots in the writer.** One shot line under each beat, so people see they plan shots too.
- **The character all the way through.** It walks, flies, points, listens, claps its board on each take, and cheers.
  It's one layer above every scene on one path, posed per frame from its parts and pivots, so it never jumps on a
  cut.
- **The library.** Creators' breakdowns and formats are a main feature: real creators, format shelves with real counts.

What it taught:
- A part's hand-over can go empty: the shelves left before the brain dump arrived, and half a second of blank paper
  read as a break. The next object has to start arriving in the last part's final frames.
- Feature asks grow the film past Apple's 30 s cap (this one is 43 s). Build the full cut, then make a 30 s App Store
  cut from it, and tell the owner which is which.

## Cut 5 (v4, 43 s): "it doesn't look good at all"

What he said: the character "needs to be bigger across the board"; the highlighted headline word "is literally wrong
... it's cut out ... why would you change that"; "Formats from real creators" should not be shelves of thumbnails but
"literally the library ... the formats and breakdowns and how the scripts had been written"; "How do you want to write
it" was "pretty bad"; the fanned "Take 2" card was not liked; the brain dump's character "gets smaller. It is not
smooth"; the writer "can be full screened"; "every intro transition should be very very smooth ... certain jerks and
jittering".

What it taught:
- The headline pill is the screenshots' pill, exactly: the word and its pill tilt together, and the line's mask clips
  only while the line moves (at rest it cut the pill's corner). Its word's colour crossfades as the pill fills.
- Show the product's real content as the product shows it: a library is breakdown cards (the reel, its format, the
  creator's own lines with their beat names), not rows of thumbnails.
- A choice screen is the options alone, big, on the page (the headline asks the question), with the app's own icons;
  no card around a card, no repeated question, no sparkle stars.
- The AI writing several drafts reads best as several rows written at once under the light; the chosen one grows
  into the whole draft, which opens into the full-screen page.
- A character's path through keyframes needs a smooth curve (monotone cubic), not an ease that stops at every key:
  that stop-go is the "jitter". Size changes ease over a second or more.
- Hand-overs one at a time: the old object fades, the new one grows, then its words come in; never both half-there.

## Cut 6 (v5 at 43 s): "no 2 characters ... we need 30sec only even everywhere"

- 30 s is the length everywhere, not only for the App Store file. To cut a longer film without re-timing every scene,
  keep the story's clock (SCENES) and give the cut its own scene ends on backbeats (CUT in timing.ts); the master maps
  each film frame to the story's time (`storyAt`) and freezes the scene there. Every scene and the character play as
  choreographed, only quicker; the part the owner loved is compressed least. Pass the character its story frame as a
  prop: Remotion clamps a frozen frame to the composition's length, so a story frame past it would stall.
- One character on screen, ever: when the app icon (which carries the character) arrives, the moving character flies
  into it and fades as the icon pops in.
- A character that only stands reads as a sticker: let it breathe when still and hop at the moments that matter.
