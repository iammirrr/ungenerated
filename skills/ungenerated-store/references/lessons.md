# What we learned, and why

Each lesson is the rule, the reason (the owner's words where there are some, dictated, so spelling is his), and where
it came from. Comma's own look appears only as the example of a general rule. The round-by-round detail behind the
screenshot and trailer lessons is in `references/screenshots-lessons.md` and `references/trailer-lessons.md`.

## Screenshots: what goes in a frame

1. **One big headline per screenshot, one feature, no small text.** "big bold avoid any sort of small texting. Small
   stuff is always bad stuff." (Comma, Nov 2026)
2. **The picture shows exactly what its headline says.** "YOU NEED SHOW THE FEATURE AND TEXT SHOULD EXACTLY BE SHOWING
   THAT CORE THING." (Comma, Nov 2026)
3. **Every screenshot advertises the core product; drop a loved one if it doesn't.** "I LOVE THIS SCREENSHOT [but]
   THIS ISN'T ADVERTISING OUR MAIN PRODUCT." (Comma, Nov 2026)
4. **The first screenshot carries the biggest statement, and gets extra variations.** "that is the only screenshot
   that I need another variation of because we have to make it stick." (Comma, Nov 2026)
5. **The real app, edited to sell: trimmed, bigger text, fewer items.** "if you're showing three beats, probably show
   two beats with a lot of bigger text"; "if you're showing four creators, show three, so they are bigger." (Comma,
   Nov 2026)
6. **Exaggerate the feature by lifting its real UI out of the screen and fading what's out of focus.** "minimize the
   screens, remove or fade away the section out of focus and improve and enhance the section in focus"; "YOU CAN USE
   THINGS OR UI COMING OUT OF SCREEN." (Comma, Nov 2026)
7. **Phones straight, slightly bigger; any tilt a few degrees.** "EXTREME TILTING BRO NO FEATURE IS SHOWING ... I LIKED
   THE STRAIGHT PHONES." (Comma, Nov 2026)
8. **Not every screenshot needs a phone.** Some show the feature as big real UI with room around it. "NO NEED TO HAVE
   PHONE IN ALL SCREENSHOTS." (Comma, Nov 2026)
9. **No props, stickers, pasted cut-outs or cards in cards; the app's character at most once, standing still.** "this
   is bloated with a lot of other elements like characters"; "a lot of fluff behind the screenshot"; "I do not like why
   the character is animating." (Comma, Nov 2026)
10. **A different real example in every screenshot.** "I don't want you to use him as uniman in every post. Obviously
    that is bad." (Comma, Nov 2026)
11. **Media keeps its true shape.** Vertical video stays tall 9:16 with its likes and handle: "it's supposed to look
    like reels." (Comma, Nov 2026)
12. **One type system across the set, with no text shadows.** "no shadow the typography need to be consistent."
    (Comma, Nov 2026)
13. **The app's own icons, never emoji stand-ins.** "I hate the icon of the stars." (Comma, Nov 2026)
14. **Exaggerate the benefit, keep the facts true.** Marketing can make a feature huge; it can't invent one. (Flamely,
    Oct 2026)

## Screenshots: how to work

15. **List the features and headlines first, let the owner edit, then design.** "I THINK FIRST IT NEED TO LIST THE
    MAJOR FEATURES AND HEADLINE WE EDIT THAT." (Comma, Nov 2026)
16. **Plan and improve the method before making.** "CAN WE PLEASE PLAN, IMPROVE THE SKILL AND THEN DO THE
    SCREENSHOTS." (Comma, Nov 2026)
17. **One screenshot at a time, by hand, judged at full size; never mass-produce a template.** Twenty-one generated
    concepts: "WHY THE FUCK THESE ARE SOOO ASSS ... CAN WE THINK ABOUT IT." (Comma, Nov 2026)
18. **Variations are completely different directions, never colour swaps.** "all three drafts that you gave me is
    supposed to have like a completely different direction." (Comma, Nov 2026)
19. **Make the board editable by clicking: move, rotate, resize, opacity, several at once.** "I'm genuinely impressed
    by the fact that I can edit stuff by just clicking on them." (Comma, Nov 2026)
20. **Automatic layout never overrides the owner's edits.** "ITS NOT RESPECTING CHANGES AND STUFF ITS GETTING
    FRUSTRATED." (Comma, Nov 2026)
21. **Screenshots on the review board are still pictures.** "why the screenshots are moving by the way." (Comma, Nov
    2026)
22. **The device follows the canvas: iPads in iPad shots, MacBooks in Mac shots, current iPhones for every size.** "IN
    IPAD SCREENSHOT USE IPADS PLEASE IN MAC USE MACBOOKS"; "make things bigger and nicer for both iPads." (Comma, Nov
    2026)
23. **Every size derives from the picked set, scaled uniformly; nothing stretches.** "I want it to be respecting the
    proportions." (Comma, Nov 2026)
24. **Languages follow the source automatically, each script with its own good font, nothing leaking from its box.**
    "it's kind of leaking out ... touching the borders"; "USE GOOD NICE FONTS." (Comma, Nov 2026)
25. **Never advertise a feature in a language it hasn't been tested in.** "I don't think it should advertise any
    script writing in a native language ... we haven't tested." (Comma, Nov 2026)
26. **A fix goes into the shared rule, and the audit runs before the owner sees anything.** "I don't want for every app
    to kind of tell these little things." (Comma, Nov 2026)

## The trailer

27. **One calm, continuous film; never the screenshots animated.** "THIS TRAILER IS LITERALLY THE SCREENSHOT COMPILED,
    I'M DISAPPOINTED TO MY CORE, IT'S NOT SMOOTH OR TRANSITIONING BETTER, OVERLY PACED, CLUTTERED WITH USELESS STUFF."
    (Comma, Nov 2026)
28. **Motion graphics, not a phone walkthrough.** "NOT AT ALL, I WANT TO MAKE MOTION GRAPHIC A LOT MORE NICER." (Comma,
    Nov 2026)
29. **No shake, flashes, wipes, wobble or spins.** "THE TRAIL NEED EXTREME REFINEMENT YOU MADE SUCH A ASS MOTION
    DESIGNING." (Comma, Nov 2026)
30. **Send a 10-second checkpoint of the opening before building the film.** Twice a whole film went out on a feel he
    rejected; the opening alone got "this one is like the best starting." (Comma, Nov 2026)
31. **Motion from the first frame; a hook in the first two seconds.** "in the first frame I would like to add a lot more
    motion." A plain big line slammed on screen also works: "Visual hook can be as simple as a big text." (Comma, Nov
    2026; CutRoom, Oct 2026)
32. **Exaggerate the AI and what the screenshots can't show.** "it doesn't do the AI stuff ... doesn't showcase the
    complete writer ... keep advertising the app that you're not covering in the screenshot." (Comma, Nov 2026)
33. **Show the product's real content as the product shows it.** A library is its real breakdown cards, not shelves
    of thumbnails: "literally the library ... how the scripts had been written." (Comma, Nov 2026)
34. **Real proof over invented graphics.** "PLEASE PREFER TO DRAW ON TOP OF REAL IMAGES." (CutRoom, Oct–Nov 2026)
35. **Headlines in the screenshots' type, and a headline that leaves is fully gone.** "the text at the top is supposed
    to be like the same as the screenshots"; a sliver of the last pill peeked out under the next. (Comma, Nov 2026)
36. **One character, big, on one smooth path through the whole film.** "no 2 characters"; "the character needs to be
    bigger across the board." (Comma, Nov 2026)
37. **Every move is smooth: curved paths, long size changes, no stop-go.** "every intro transition should be very very
    smooth ... certain jerks and jittering." (Comma, Nov 2026)
38. **Check every hand-over as a pair of frames.** Four of six cuts first dropped something between one part's last
    frame and the next one's first. (Comma, Nov 2026)
39. **30 seconds, everywhere.** "we need 30sec only even everywhere." (Comma, Nov 2026)
40. **Promise only what the app does.** It couldn't edit video, so the film never says "edit". (Comma, Nov 2026)
41. **A variation is a different visual idea, not the same edit with blur added.** "the variation you created is
    basically useless because you added a lot of blur and motion blur." (CutRoom, Oct 2026)
42. **Sound is sparse and sits under everything.** "SFX FEELS SO FORCED"; "make the music ... lower ... its too loud."
    (CutRoom, Oct–Nov 2026)
43. **Check frames of the encoded file before sending.** A blur pass that silently failed once shipped the previous
    cut. (Comma, Nov 2026)

## ASO, banners and the rest

44. **Research each region's real search words before localising; use a real keyword tool.** "if we are localizing for
    say India should we use their language? or should we look for research in their region?" (Comma, Nov 2026)
45. **Banners follow the current App Store look, and Claude makes them.** "BANNER SHOULD BE MADE BY CLAUDE ICON SHOULD
    BE WRITTEN DRAFTS TO CODEX"; "BANNER IS LOOK NEW APPSTORE STUFF." (Comma, Nov 2026)
46. **Header art and thumbnails: the brand's own background, uncluttered, the subject and logo big, checked square
    and vertical.** "MAKE SURE ITS NOT TOO MUCH CLUTTERED"; "it should look good on the square and also ... vertical."
    (CutRoom, Oct 2026)
47. **Posts that show design work show the real process, and carry no branding until you're ready to market.** "Not
    these dramatic effects ... it doesn't show the exact process"; "I did not want to market my app yet." (Comma,
    Oct–Nov 2026)
48. **Build an audience that cares about the problem, not "download my app".** "I just want to make sure that I build
    an audience that kind of care for their time." (Flamely, Oct 2026)
49. **Every release ships with its changelog.** The store's "What's New" and the public changelog are updated together:
    "remeber all rhe changlog also updes." (Flamely, Nov 2026)

## Working with the owner

50. **Log every note first, then change; fold the lesson into this skill.** "all the learnings that you did from this,
    you have to update in the skill." (Comma, Nov 2026)
51. **Never ask the owner to pick something he can't see; show every option at full size.** (Comma, Oct–Nov 2026)
52. **Never test the editor on the owner's real edits; use a copy.** Edits are his work. (Comma, Nov 2026)
