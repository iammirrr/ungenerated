# App research

## Where to look

1. **A connected design library** if one is available in this session (for example the Appllama or
   Mobbin MCP servers). Search by journey ("onboarding", "paywall", "permission ask"), walk the top
   apps screen by screen, and save the screens you learn from to the board's research folder.
2. **Screenshots the person gives you.** They are the strongest signal of what they like.
3. **This skill's notes**, `references/patterns.md`, distilled from studying the top-grossing
   onboarding and paywall flows. Use them when nothing is connected.

## What to take

- The **order** of screens, what each asks, where the account and the paywall sit.
- **Mechanics**: a hands-on moment in the opening, the answer-is-the-button, a commitment before
  the price, a trial timeline.
- **Pacing**: how many beats, how long each holds, when the button appears.

## What never to take

- Their visuals: colours, illustrations, mascots, layouts copied one to one.
- Dark patterns: fake countdowns, pre-ticked upsells, hidden prices, confirm-shaming.

## Output

For every section of the flow, two to five research cards on the board's Research view:
`{ title: 'Brainrot: the opening', took: 'One idea per beat, connected by the same hero.' }`.
The `took` line is the intent you borrowed, in one sentence. Screenshots are optional and stay
private: other apps' screens are their artwork.

## How to study a flow

1. List every screen in order with what it asks and how you move on.
2. Mark the one moment you would remember tomorrow. That is the mechanic to learn.
3. Note what it does not do (no login first, no Continue on single picks).
4. Write the card. Move on; three good apps beat ten skimmed ones.
