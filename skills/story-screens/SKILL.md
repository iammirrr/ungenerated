# Story screens

The story turns answers into a reason to care. It has an angle and depth; it is never a list of
facts.

## The arc (4–5 beats)

1. **You.** A profile from their answers: their rhythm, their pattern, with the brand's hero
   inside it (not a stock person).
2. **The honest part.** Their own number, felt: the little losses scattered through their day,
   gathering into one block; the year counting up in red; a stamp that lands once. One heading.
   The sum is shown small underneath so it can be checked.
3. **Scale.** A year, then ten years, in the same place. Bigger units land harder than small ones
   ("2,600 hours" beats "32 workdays"); the maths stays the same.
4. **The good news.** It starts exactly where the bad news ended and fills it back in, row by row,
   in the brand's hopeful colour. A number that counts up and lands.
5. **How.** The three or four ways the app delivers it, each one big motion on the ground with one
   bold line, in tabs or beats. No cards with paragraphs.

## Rules

- **One visual per beat** that does the explaining. The heading names it; nothing else is needed.
- **Their numbers, honest maths.** Exaggerate scale, never facts. No invented statistics.
- **Hopeful after honest.** Never end on the bad news.
- **Continue appears after a moment** (about 1.5 s) so the first beat is seen; a tap moves on.
- **No segmented bars at the top** of story beats.

## A stamp that looks like a stamp

Two nested borders, uppercase, heavy weight, rotated a few degrees, ink grain from a noise mask,
dropped from 2.3× scale in 230 ms with one rigid haptic and a 240 ms shake of the page. Once.

## Anti-patterns

- A stat card grid.
- "Did you know…?" facts that are not theirs.
- Persona illustrations that could belong to any app.

## Merges and numbers

- **A merge is one shape growing.** When pieces gather (minutes into an hour, slips into a block),
  one block grows from where they collect and each piece slides into its edge and becomes part of
  it, one after another. Never fade the pieces out and pop a finished block in.
- **No lines drawn across the content** to show time passing; let the pieces appear in time order.
- **Keep the margins.** Pieces that gather inside a container stop at its inner padding, left and
  right alike.
- **Make the default dramatic, keep the answer theirs.** Default the estimate to the answer that
  makes the story land (two hours a day reads as ten hours a week back), and always use what the
  person picks.

## Holding it together

A story is only as good as its joins. Plan them with `continuity`: what carries from each beat to
the next, and which way the story moves.
