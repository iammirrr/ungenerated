# The thread sheet

Fill this in for a part of the flow before animating any of it. One row per change of screen.

| # | From | Last frame (what is on screen, where the eye is) | Baton | First frame of the next | To | Move | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | | | | |

**Baton:** the one element that is on screen in both frames. It may change size, place or form, but
it is the same element (or two copies crossing on one path).

**Move:** carry, zoom, morph, camera, cover, step, words, or cut (a cut needs a reason).

## A worked example: an opening for a time tracker, told as one workday

| # | From | Last frame | Baton | First frame | To | Move |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Grey start | the app's mark, grey, app icons scattered round it | the mark | the mark lit, the icons going quiet | Focus | words swap; the mark lights in place |
| 2 | Focus | the mark at the top, a focus ring drawn | the mark | the mark small at the top, a day card below | Plan | camera pans down; the mark shrinks to its slot |
| 3 | Plan | the day card with planned blocks | the card | the same card, a tracked lane filling | Track | none: the card stays, a line moves down it |
| 4 | Track | the full day card | the card | the card on a phone's glass | Phone | the card shrinks onto the glass, then the phone appears around it |
| 5 | Phone | the phone, card upright on its glass | the phone | a laptop, the card on its screen | Laptop | morph: the phone turns and grows into the lid; the base slides in |
| 6 | Laptop | the laptop, camera moving in | the card | the card becoming a ring of the day | Result | camera goes into the screen; the card cross-fades into the ring |
| 7 | Result | the ring with the mark in its centre | the mark | the mark large, the last line and the button | End | the mark lifts out of the ring to the hero place |

What made it hold together: the same card from row 3 to row 6, the same mark at the start and
the end, the camera only ever moving down or in, and a ground that never changed.
