# AI tells

Each tell is a named check: what it looks like, and what to do instead. One tell is enough for
someone to say "this looks generated". Go screen by screen, light and dark, and name every tell you
find by its number in the report ("T2 on the library card").

A tell is only a tell when nothing asked for it. If the owner picked something on this list, keep
it and note why.

## Surfaces and decoration

| # | Tell | What it looks like | Instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Pills inside text | Rounded capsules round words in a sentence, a heading or a row ("Built with `AI`") | Plain words. Weight or the one accent for emphasis. A pill only for a real control, or a label that has to sit over a pattern |
| T2 | Nuggets on cards | Little badges, tags, stamps and counts sprinkled on every card ("New", "AI", "Typed just now", "3 min", "4 of 15") | The card holds its content. If state matters, one state icon. Show a number only if it helps them decide something |
| T3 | Boxed or double-outlined cards | A border plus a shadow plus an inner border; cards inside cards | Independent elements on the ground, or one soft borderless surface |
| T4 | Shadows behind icons, logos and emoji | A drop shadow or halo under every glyph and app icon | Flat on the surface. Shadows only for things that really lift (a dragged item, a sheet) |
| T5 | Glows | A halo behind the hero, glowing borders, a glowing mascot, an "AI aura" | Nothing behind. For AI at work, a thin light running on the edge of the box that's already there |
| T6 | Generic gradients and violet | Purple-to-blue, pink-to-orange, gradient text, a ground that changes colour between screens | The picked colours, flat grounds. If there is a gradient, one soft one, the same on every screen of a flow |
| T7 | The AI kit | Indigo or violet accents, the trendy serif-plus-grotesk pairing every generator reaches for, brown and beige everywhere | The app's own kit (`ungenerated-foundation` › design-kit (references/design-kit.md)) |
| T8 | Busy grounds | Dot, plus or grid patterns, particles, smoke, shines, sweeping lines | A calm ground. One owned pattern at most, slow, and not the one AI tools default to |
| T9 | Craft textures | Paper, tape, handwriting fonts, sticker outlines | Soft, premium app surfaces |
| T10 | Sparkle icons | ✨ or a sparkles glyph standing for AI | The provider's real mark, the app's own character or tool (a pencil), or no icon |
| T11 | Emoji bullets | ✅ 🚀 💡 as list markers or in UI text | Icons from the one line set, or no marker |
| T12 | Decorative icons | An icon beside every heading, a header icon that does nothing | None. An icon earns its place by being tapped or by telling state |
| T13 | Stand-in brand icons | A phone emoji for the App Store, a drawn tile for Google Docs, a generic camera for Instagram | The official artwork (the App Store lookup for apps, the press kit for marks, Apple's own badges). Not found? Say so and ask |

## Layout and structure

| # | Tell | What it looks like | Instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| T14 | Eyebrows | A small uppercase label above every heading | The heading alone |
| T15 | Two-tone giant headlines | A poster-size heading with half of it in another colour or a gradient | One size that fits the screen; one italic or accent word at most |
| T16 | Everything centred | Every block, list and paragraph centred | Read from the left. Centre a single hero line or an empty state's one action |
| T17 | Identical card grids | A 2 × 2 or 3 × 3 grid of the same card (icon, title, line) | A list, one hero, or a real picture of the thing |
| T18 | Tabs nobody asked for | A segmented control splitting one screen into views ("For you · New · Browse") | One view. Tabs only for two real, equally used modes, or to fold two related cards into one |
| T19 | Step counters | "Step 2 of 4" | A thin progress bar, or screens that get shorter |
| T20 | Small text | Captions, sub-labels, footnotes, grey fine print | Cut it, or make it a real line at a readable size |
| T21 | Dressed as another app | A date header or "Today" on a list that can't move between days; a dashboard where a list would do | Name the screen for what it lists. Model the real job |
| T22 | Coloured state rows | Rows tinted blue, orange or green to mean a status | Neutral rows and a state icon that reads at a glance |
| T23 | Big-text cards | A card that is only one big line of text | Text on the ground, or a real visual that explains |
| T24 | The landing template | Marquees, logo walls, pins, a feature carousel with dots | Plain pages; before and after side by side; parts shown alone |

## Copy

| # | Tell | What it looks like | Instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| T25 | Clipped punchy fragments | "Write. Film. Post." "No fluff. Just results." | One plain sentence a person would say out loud |
| T26 | Buzzwords | unlock, supercharge, seamless, elevate, effortless, journey, empower, AI-powered | Say what it does, with a specific noun |
| T27 | Corny promises | "Stop posting into the void" | The promise said plainly |
| T28 | Invented jargon | Feature names that mean nothing yet ("Draft leak", "Signal dashboard") | Words a newcomer understands; every label says what tapping it does |
| T29 | Persona labels | "The Shapeless Poster", "You're a Night Owl Creator" | Talk about their time and their work |
| T30 | Text for the sake of text | A caption that says what the picture shows; a sub-line under every heading | Remove it. An empty moment is a valid result |
| T31 | Generic headings | A heading that could sit in any app ("Your personalised plan is ready") | This app's words and the person's numbers |
| T32 | Three adjectives and exclamation marks | "Fast, simple and powerful!" | One fact |

| T33 | Fake stats | "10,000+ creators", "Save 5 hours a week" with no source | The person's own numbers, with the maths checkable |

## Motion

| # | Tell | What it looks like | Instead |
|---|---|---|---|
| T36 | Everything staggers in | Every element of every screen rising in a wave | App screens appear like an app's; motion for achievements, popups and moments of change |
| T37 | Buzz | Content bouncing back and forth, a counter bumped repeatedly, jitter | Settle once. Measure it frame by frame |
| T38 | Flips | A flat badge, card or character turning over in 3D or mirroring | A swap: the old shrinks away, the new springs in |
| T39 | Text swaps | Words sliding and blurring out while new ones slide in | Crossfade in place, or no motion |
| T40 | Confetti for nothing | Celebration on a tap that achieved nothing | Save it for a real achievement, or drop it |
| T41 | AI spinners | A spinning circle, a comet, a dark trail, a pop-up over the text | The light on the edge of the exact card, then the words written in |
| T42 | A mascot on every tap | The character reacting to buttons it has nothing to do with | It reacts to real moments and to being tapped |
| T43 | Pop-ins | 0 to 1 opacity in a frame; a finished card appearing at full size | Grow, rise or carry from where it came from |

Motion details and the right numbers are in `ungenerated-motion`.

## Make them lint rules

When a tell comes back twice, turn it into a check a script can run on every screen, so it can't
come back a third time. Examples:

- icon names `sparkles`, `wand-sparkles`, and ✨ in source
- `text-transform: uppercase` with letter spacing directly above a heading (eyebrows)
- violet and indigo hex values; `linear-gradient(` outside the allowed ground
- `box-shadow` or `filter: drop-shadow` on images, icons and emoji
- `Step \d of \d` in copy
- emoji ranges in UI strings
- the buzzword list in copy
- persona phrasings ("You're a … creator")

Keep the rules in the project, next to the owner's taste notes, and run them before every round.
