# Before you show a screen

Tick each line for this screen, in light and dark.

- [ ] The heading is 2–6 words and specific to this app.
- [ ] At most one line under it; no paragraph.
- [ ] One accent, used once in the heading.
- [ ] No gradient that is not a soft ground; no glow behind the hero.
- [ ] Every icon is from the one set or a real app's artwork.
- [ ] Corners nest; radii follow the scale.
- [ ] Nothing important is cut off; no device is cut off.
- [ ] Every number traces to the person's answers or says it is an example.
- [ ] Motion: no pop, no swap, no bounce on content; leaving is faster than arriving.
- [ ] Captured mid-motion and at rest; looked at the frames.
- [ ] Matches every standing rule from the reviewer.
