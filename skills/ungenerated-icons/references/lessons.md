# What we learned, and why

Each lesson is the rule, the reason (the owner's words where there are some, dictated, so spelling is his), and where
it came from. Comma's own look (its palette, its clapperboard character) appears only as the example of a general rule.

## Real brands

1. **A real brand is always its original artwork, Apple's App Store icon included.** Never a lookalike, a generic
   glyph, an emoji or a drawn approximation. "if I write App Store, it is giving me … false icon … I do not want that
   for any application"; "App Store is supposed to have App Store original icon". (Comma, Oct and Nov 2026; Flamely,
   Oct 2026: "the app icons supposed to be 100% correct")
2. **If the original isn't in your library, go and get it; don't substitute.** "my library doesn't have Blender logo,
   then fucking download it. That is the point." (CutRoom, Nov 2026)
3. **Real characters are the real ones too.** A redrawn Duolingo owl read as wrong at once: "not even Duolingo
   original mascot … it could have gotten Duolingo mascot anywhere". (CutRoom, Nov 2026)
4. **Real icons big enough to read.** "the Figma icon, the Claude, ChatGPT icon … supposed to be bigger". (Flamely,
   Oct 2026)
5. **No shadow behind any icon, logo or emoji.** "I do not like the shadow behind the TikTok, Instagram"; "every icon
   have a shadow which I do not like". The soft contact shadow inside an illustrated icon's artwork is a different
   thing and stayed. (Comma, Oct and Nov 2026)
6. **Several platforms at once read best as one overlapping mark,** each cut out with a thin ring in the ground colour.
   "Instagram and TikTok overlapping logo, do that." (Comma, Oct 2026)
7. **Check the licence of every mark and set you ship.** Third-party icons in onboarding are an App Review 5.2.1 risk,
   and open-licence sets (Lucide ISC, Fluent MIT) need their notices in the app. Other companies' mascots need their
   fan-content terms checked before marketing. (Pip, Oct 2026; CutRoom, Nov 2026)

## The list

8. **Build the icon list from the code: open every screen, record every icon it draws and its size.** Comma's first
   list was written from memory and missed the labels, the credits and the sun: "the list was not properly made to
   begin with … Please analyze deeply." (Comma, Nov 2026)
9. **Make the complete list before generating anything.** Asking for icons in pieces meant the set never matched.
   (Comma, Nov 2026)
10. **Placeholders stand in until the owner's file exists,** and the importer swaps them by name. Design keeps moving
    while the icons are made, and one approved file can serve several names. (Comma, Nov 2026)

## Line or illustrated

11. **Small UI stays line icons; illustrated icons go where the icon is an object or a moment with room.** "the
    navigation bar completely looks fucked" with illustrated icons; "small stuff looks better with the icons that we
    currently have, but the leagues icon, the sun icon … script or camera … looks better". (Comma, Nov 2026)
12. **Don't add an icon that only repeats what the tab or title already says.** "What a thing is doesn't need an icon
    when the tab already says it." (Comma, Oct 2026)
13. **Use small icons instead of word labels on content layers** (a voice wave, a screen, a camera). Words like
    "B-roll" and "voice" on every layer were noise. (Comma, Oct 2026)
14. **In a grouped list, the group's icon shows once, in the header.** "why there are like fucking four icons for the
    freaking draft". (Comma, Nov 2026)
15. **A camera view is a plain viewfinder, never an illustrated person.** "I do not like this person image." (Comma,
    Oct 2026)

## Meaning

16. **One meaning per icon, across the whole app.** "idea stands for bulb in my game". (Comma, Nov 2026)
17. **States get icons distinct enough to read without words, and each object type gets its own state set.**
    "different icons that are like differentiator … psychological"; "IDEAS SUPPOSED TO HAVE SEPARATE ICON SET".
    (Comma, Nov 2026)
18. **A state is an icon, never a coloured row.** From the owner's own summary of the look: "A script's state
    is an icon, never a coloured row." (Comma, Nov 2026)
19. **Pick icons that fit the meaning, and check their orientation.** "use a better icon than the brain on 'My own
    thoughts'"; a calendar icon was drawn the wrong way round. (Flamely, Oct 2026)

## Style

20. **Offer style options, get a pick, then generate.** "codex can generate images, and Claude can't … it will give
    them a couple of options to choose from design style … if they confirm, he will generate". (Comma, Nov 2026)
21. **The illustrated set takes its style from the app's character, and the main tasks look identical everywhere they
    appear.** "needs to be consistent across the board … I like the style of the character". (Comma, Nov 2026)
22. **The look that won was flat and quiet:** clean filled silhouettes, softly rounded corners, broad very subtle
    gradients, three brand colours, no strokes, readable at 32 px. "it looks absolutely bangers." (Comma, Nov 2026)
23. **Clay, matte plastic, inflated, puffy, bevelled and glossy icons were rejected outright.** The first batch came back clay and
    was thrown away whole; the flat style that won is the opposite. (Comma, Nov 2026)
24. **People, hands and faces in a subject are drawn faceless, and objects never get eyes or limbs.** It keeps the set
    as objects and leaves the face to the character. (Comma, Nov 2026)
25. **An open-licence glossy emoji set can beat generating,** for answer cards and emoji-like moments. Fluent emoji on
    answer cards: "I really like this screen, amazing." (Comma, Oct 2026)
26. **Illustrations that explain something are crafted little scenes, and each must read when still at its smallest
    size.** Two diagrams that looked the same at small size: "These are looking really ass." (Comma, Oct 2026)

## The brief

27. **Claude writes the brief and the list; the image model makes the pictures.** Claude can't make images, and a
    hand-drawn SVG standing in for the real set wastes a round. (Comma, Nov 2026)
28. **One style paragraph, word for word, plus two references (the character and one approved icon), then one icon at
    a time: "Next: `file-name`: subject".** This is how the approved set was made. (Comma, Nov 2026)
29. **Transparent 1024 × 1024 PNGs, named exactly, about 78% fill, imported by a script.** The exact name is what lets
    the script replace each placeholder on the board and in the app at once. (Comma, Nov 2026)
30. **Redo weak icons by name, with the reason.** Regenerating a whole set to fix two loses the ones already approved.
    (Comma, Nov 2026)
31. **Claude makes the banners; the icons go to the image model as written drafts.** "BANNER SHOULD BE MADE BY CLAUDE
    ICON SHOULD BE WRITTEN DRAFTS TO CODEX". (Comma, Nov 2026)

## The character and the app icon

32. **Animate the owner's character drawing; never redesign it.** New poses come from moving his parts about their
    joints; missing parts are drawn in his palette; any blush is crisp, never a soft glow: "we do not want soft, like
    soft blush". (Comma, Oct 2026)
33. **When the character changes, it changes everywhere at once, the app icon included, with no fallback.** An old
    octopus surviving in one corner: "FUCK IS OCTOPUS". (Comma, Oct 2026)
34. **The app icon follows the character.** When the clapperboard replaced the octopus, the icon went the same day.
    (Comma, Oct 2026)

## Working with the owner

35. **Never ask the owner to pick something he can't see.** Put every option at its real size on one page, light and
    dark. "the paywall, I want you to show me, like I can't see". (Comma, Oct 2026)
36. **Log each verdict first, rejected styles included, then change the set.** He shouldn't have to reject clay twice.
    (Comma, Oct–Nov 2026)
37. **Every icon approved on the board lands in the app in the same round.** The importer writes both, so they can't
    drift. (Comma, Nov 2026)
